# English terms dictation writes in Latin, in their usual spelling. Ordinary English words (pull, merge, deploy,
# staging, master…) come from the English frequency list; this list holds what that list lacks: names, brands,
# acronyms and jargon, plus phrases.
#
# Canonical|heard|heard…  — heard forms are how the term comes out in Cyrillic when its letters do not say it
# (acronyms spelled out, odd pronunciations). Everything else is matched by sound from the canonical spelling.
# A term whose heard form is a common word of the language (Python → питон, CUDA → куда) does not belong here:
# such words stay as spoken unless the user adds them to their own glossary.
